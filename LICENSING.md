## GPLv3

The GPLv3 terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Under the GPLv3 you may use, study, modify and share OpenPLC Runtime freely. If you distribute it, or a product that contains it, you must make the complete corresponding source code available under the GPLv3 to the people who receive it, including the PLC programs and drivers that run combined with it.

## Commercial licence

The commercial licence lets you use OpenPLC Runtime without the GPLv3 obligations. If you are using OpenPLC Runtime in a commercial setting, you may need a commercial licence.

Licences can be bought from the OpenPLC Editor (desktop or web) when you upload a program to the device. A licence is bound to the hardware it was bought for.

The runtime never restricts anything: it runs the same way with or without a commercial licence. When a target has no commercial licence, the OpenPLC Editor shows a notice that the program runs under the GPLv3.

## Third-party components

Some components keep their own licences, for example the S7comm plugin, which is LGPLv3 or later to match the Snap7 library it links.
